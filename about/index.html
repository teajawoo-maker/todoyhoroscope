About Today Horoscope

Who runs Today Horoscope, why it exists, and how the daily Chinese zodiac readings and guides are made and corrected.

Today Horoscope is a small English-language site about the Chinese zodiac. It offers a short daily reflection for each of the 12 signs, plain-language guides to the signs and their years, and a tool to find your sign from your birth date.

Who runs this site

Today Horoscope is independently run by Wozo, an indie maker and digital nomad who builds small, useful web tools. Wozo decides what the site covers and answers reader mail at teajawoo@gmail.com. The site is not affiliated with any temple, school of astrology, or commercial reading service.

Why it exists

Wozo made the site to offer a simple daily Chinese zodiac reading with no sign-up.

How the content is made

  • Guides and sign pages were drafted with an automated writing system. Wozo publishes and maintains them and fixes reported errors.
  • Daily readings are generated automatically each day by a script from a library of sign-specific reflection passages. Each daily page shows the date it was updated.
  • An automated check runs before each daily update to confirm that the pages are complete and still carry their safety notes.

For entertainment only

All readings and guides on this site are for entertainment and personal reflection only. They do not predict the future and are not medical, legal, financial, or relationship advice. Please see the Disclaimer.

Corrections and updates

If something is wrong, email teajawoo@gmail.com with the page address. Confirmed errors are corrected and the page's update date changes.

How the site is funded

The site is supported by Google AdSense ads. Advertisers have no say in the content. See the Disclaimer and Privacy Policy for details.
