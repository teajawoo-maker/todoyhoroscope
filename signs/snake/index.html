Snake Chinese Zodiac: Snake Years 1929–2025, Exact Start Dates, Fire Element & Matches

Snake years from 1929 to 2025 with exact Lunar New Year start dates, the Snake's fixed yin Fire element, its Ox and Rooster trine, Monkey pairing and Pig clash.

Published October 15, 2026 · Updated October 26, 2026

Sixth in the sequence, the Snake takes the branch 巳 (sì). Its double hour runs from 9 a.m. to 11 a.m., the time when, by tradition, the sun warms the ground and snakes leave their holes. Its fixed element is Fire, yin, and the month of the Snake is the fourth lunar month, usually landing in May or June.

Snake compatibility in the classical system

The Snake sits in the Metal trine (巳酉丑) alongside the Ox and the Rooster. Its Liu He match is the Monkey (巳申). Its clash, six branches away, is the Pig (巳亥).

Snake in folklore and culture

Stories differ on how the Snake beat the Horse. In the British Library's version the Horse is a very good swimmer, but just as it is about to cross the finish line the Snake slithers out of its mane and takes sixth place.[2]

The version Hong Kong's Education Bureau uses in schools has the Horse swim across with no problem; as it shakes itself dry, the Snake suddenly appears, the frightened Horse jumps backwards, and the Snake takes sixth place while the Horse becomes seventh.[3]

Snake figures have been made as part of zodiac sets for centuries. The Metropolitan Museum of Art holds an 11th-century Song-dynasty snake in qingbai porcelain from Jingdezhen with brown iron splashes[4], and a 13th-century grey earthenware snake with pigment dated to the Jin or Yuan dynasty.[5]

The month of the Snake and the hour of the Snake are separate from the Snake year; a full chart looks at all three. In both race stories the Snake finishes just ahead of the Horse.
