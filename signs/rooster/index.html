Rooster compatibility in the classical system

The Rooster joins the Ox and the Snake in the Metal trine (巳酉丑). Its Liu He match is the Dragon (辰酉). Its clash partner is the Rabbit (卯酉).

Rooster in folklore and culture

The Rooster does not win its place alone. In the race as Hong Kong's Education Bureau tells it to pupils, the Rooster, the Goat and the Monkey work together to make a raft, and the Emperor rewards that cooperation by making the Rooster tenth.[2] The Victoria and Albert Museum's list of the twelve also names this animal the rooster.[3]

The U.S. Postal Service's 2017 Year of the Rooster stamp, the tenth in its Celebrating Lunar New Year series, shows a rooster on a red hongbao envelope. Its announcement notes that red symbolizes luck in Chinese culture, that rooster imagery is often used to ward off evil spirits, and that the Rooster year ran from January 28, 2017 to February 15, 2018.[4]

The Metropolitan Museum of Art catalogues a Kangxi-period porcelain zodiac figure of a rooster, about 15 cm tall, painted in enamels on the biscuit at Jingdezhen in the late 17th or early 18th century.[5]

English sources also say Chicken; 雞 covers both.
