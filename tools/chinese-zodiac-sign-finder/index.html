How the result is calculated

The tool uses 2020 as a Rat-year reference and moves through the 12-animal sequence one year at a time. Adding or subtracting 12 returns to the same cycle label. It does not send or store the year you enter.

Why January and February need another check

The Chinese zodiac year follows the Lunar New Year boundary, not January 1. If the birthday falls before Lunar New Year, the preceding cycle sign may apply. Verify the complete date with a dated calendar table before saving or sharing the result.
