Rat Chinese Zodiac: Rat Years 1924–2020, Exact Start Dates, Water Element & Matches

Every Rat year from 1924 to 2020 with its exact Lunar New Year start date, the Rat's fixed Water element, its Monkey and Dragon trine, Ox pairing and Horse clash.

The Rat opens the twelve-animal cycle. Its Earthly Branch is 子 (zǐ), the branch that also names the double hour around midnight, 11 p.m. to 1 a.m. Its fixed element is Water and, as the first branch, it is yang.

Rat years with exact start and end dates

Rat years have started as early as January 24 (1936) and as late as February 19 (1996). Someone born on February 10, 1996, therefore counts as a Wood Pig, not a Fire Rat, even though the Gregorian year already says 1996. For 2008 the line falls on February 7; for 2020 it falls on January 25.[1]

Rat compatibility in the classical system

In the San He system the Rat belongs to the Water trine with the Monkey and the Dragon, the three branches 申子辰. Its Liu He, or one-to-one harmony, is with the Ox (子丑). Its clash partner sits directly across the wheel: the Horse (子午). Pairing tables like these describe how the branches combine in calendar astrology. They say nothing about whether two actual people treat each other well.

Rat in folklore and culture

In the version of the Great Race told by the British Library, the cat, afraid of water, rides across the river on the Ox's back and the Rat climbs aboard too. Halfway over, the Rat pushes the cat into the river, and as the Ox is about to step ashore the Rat leaps over its shoulders to win. The cat reaches the bank too late for any place and vows to be the Rat's enemy forever.[2]

The branch 子 is far older than its animal. The earliest Chinese writing, from around 1250 BCE, already counts days with a twelve-term cycle, but scholar Adam Smith notes that the familiar link between that cycle and a list of animals is not attested in its first thousand years of use.[3]

If you were born near midnight, note that the hour of the Rat straddles two calendar days, which matters for anyone building a full Four Pillars chart.
