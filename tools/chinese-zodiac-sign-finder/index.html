How the result is worked out

A Chinese zodiac year starts on Lunar New Year's Day, which moves between January 21 and February 20. The finder holds the start date of every lunar year from 1924 to 2032, taken from the Hong Kong Observatory's yearly Gregorian-Lunar conversion tables and cross-checked against a second lunar calendar library; all 109 dates agree. If your birthday falls before that year's start date, you belong to the previous year's animal. Lunar New Year's Day itself already counts as the new year.

The element comes from the year's Heavenly Stem, which follows the last digit of the lunar year: 4 and 5 are Wood, 6 and 7 Fire, 8 and 9 Earth, 0 and 1 Metal, 2 and 3 Water. That is why 1984 was a Wood Rat and 1996 a Fire Rat.

Lunar New Year or Lichun?

Most calendars, placemats and horoscope columns switch animals at Lunar New Year, and so does this finder. BaZi (Four Pillars) charts usually switch at Lichun, the solar term that opens spring, which the Hong Kong Observatory lists on February 4 in 2026. Take a birthday of February 10, 2026: Lichun had passed, so a BaZi chart starts the Fire Horse year, but Lunar New Year did not arrive until February 17, so by the Lunar New Year rule the person is still a Wood Snake. Both answers follow a real convention; just know which one a source is using.

Frequently asked questions

Why does this give a different animal than a year-only chart?

Year-only charts assume the animal changes on January 1. It does not; it changes on Lunar New Year. Anyone born in January or in the first weeks of February should trust a date-based result.

I was born on Lunar New Year's Day. Which animal am I?

The new one. The start date in the table is the first day of the new lunar year.

I was born outside Asia, close to midnight on the boundary. Does that matter?

Possibly. The table follows the Hong Kong Observatory's calendar for Hong Kong. A birth late on the evening before Lunar New Year in the Americas or Europe can fall on Lunar New Year's Day in Hong Kong, so if you were born within a day of the boundary, compare your local birth date with the date in Hong Kong.

My year is outside 1924 to 2032.

The Hong Kong Observatory publishes conversion tables from 1901 to 2100. Find your birth year there, look up the date marked as the first day of the first lunar month, and compare it with your birthday.

Is the zodiac sign a personality test?

No. The animal is a calendar label with a long cultural history. Treat descriptions of it as folklore and conversation, not as a judgment about who you are.
