Ox Chinese Zodiac: Ox Years 1925–2021, Exact Start Dates, Earth Element & Matches

All Ox years from 1925 to 2021 with exact Lunar New Year start dates, the Ox's fixed yin Earth element, its Snake and Rooster trine, Rat pairing and Goat clash.

Published October 15, 2026 · Updated October 26, 2026

Second in line, the Ox carries the branch 丑 (chǒu) and governs the double hour from 1 a.m. to 3 a.m. Its fixed element is Earth, and as an even-numbered branch it is classed as yin. The Chinese word behind the sign, 牛 niú, covers cattle in general, including the water buffalo, so the English label is a translator's choice.

Ox years with exact start and end dates

Ox years are spread unusually widely: 1925 began on January 24, while 1985 did not begin until February 20, one of the latest possible starts. A baby born on February 15, 1985 is still a Wood Rat. In 1961 the cutoff was February 15, and in 2021 it was February 12.[1]

Ox compatibility in the classical system

Within the Metal trine (巳酉丑) the Ox is grouped with the Snake and the Rooster. Its single Liu He match is the Rat, the animal that, in legend, rode on its back. Its six-clash opposite is the Goat (丑未), the other yin Earth sign, which sits six places away.

Ox in folklore and culture

In the British Library's telling of the river race, the Ox carries the cat across on its back, with the Rat riding along. Just before the Ox reaches the far bank, the Rat jumps over its shoulders and scurries to the finish, leaving the Ox in second place, ahead of the Tiger.[2]

The animal changes as the calendar travels. Vietnam's version of the cycle uses the water buffalo instead of the ox, as the Victoria and Albert Museum points out.[3]

Whichever English word you use for 牛, the branch and the dates in the table stay the same.
