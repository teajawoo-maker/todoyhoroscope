Tiger Chinese Zodiac: Tiger Years 1926–2022, Exact Start Dates, Wood Element & Matches

Tiger years from 1926 to 2022 with exact Lunar New Year cutoffs, the Tiger's fixed yang Wood element, its Horse and Dog trine, Pig pairing and Monkey clash.

Published October 15, 2026 · Updated October 26, 2026

The Tiger is the third animal and holds the branch 寅 (yín), which rules the pre-dawn double hour from 3 a.m. to 5 a.m. Its fixed element is Wood with yang polarity. In the solar-month scheme 寅 is also the first month of spring, beginning at the solar term Lichun, which the Hong Kong Observatory lists on February 4 in 2026.

Tiger years with exact start and end dates

That link to Lichun is where the Tiger causes the most confusion. In 1974 the Tiger year began on January 23, so a January 30, 1974 birthday is a Wood Tiger under the Lunar New Year rule. In 1950 it began on February 17, so February 10, 1950 still belongs to the Earth Ox. In 2022 the date was February 1.[1]

Tiger compatibility in the classical system

The Tiger anchors the Fire trine (寅午戌) together with the Horse and the Dog. Its Liu He partner is the Pig (寅亥). The sign directly opposite, and therefore its six-clash, is the Monkey (寅申).

Tiger in folklore and culture

In the British Library's telling of the river race, the Tiger comes in close behind the Ox, in third place; her strength is what carried her through the strong currents that dragged at her fur.[2]

The Tiger is one of the animals that changed as the calendar spread. The Victoria and Albert Museum notes that the Turkic peoples of Central Asia replaced the tiger with a leopard, adapting the cycle to animals they knew.[3]

If your birthday falls between Lichun and Lunar New Year, a BaZi practitioner and a party placemat may give you different animals; both are following a real convention.
