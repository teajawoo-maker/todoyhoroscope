Dog compatibility in the classical system

San He groups the Dog with the Tiger and the Horse in the Fire trine (寅午戌). Its Liu He partner is the Rabbit (卯戌). Its six-clash opposite is the Dragon (辰戌).

Dog in folklore and culture

In the Hong Kong Education Bureau's school version of the race, the Dog turns up eleventh. Asked by the Jade Emperor why it is so late, it admits that the river was so clean it stopped to play in it for a while.[2]

The U.S. Postal Service marked the Dog year that ran from February 16, 2018 to February 4, 2019 with the 11th stamp of its 12-stamp Celebrating Lunar New Year series. Kam Mak's painting shows lucky bamboo beside a lozenge-shaped red paper decoration, and the design reuses Clarence Lee's cut-paper dog and the character for dog in grass-style calligraphy by Lau Bun.[3]

The Metropolitan Museum of Art catalogues a Kangxi-period porcelain zodiac figure of a dog, 14.3 cm tall, painted in enamels on the biscuit at Jingdezhen in the late 17th or early 18th century.[4]

In 60-year terms, 2018 was 戊戌, an Earth Dog year, and the next 戊戌 arrives in 2078.
