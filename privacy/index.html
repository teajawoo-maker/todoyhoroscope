Privacy Policy

How Today Horoscope handles data, including Google AdSense advertising cookies, third-party vendors, and how to opt out of personalized ads.

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit todoyhoroscope.com ("Today Horoscope", "we", "us"), why, and the choices you have. Questions can be sent to teajawoo@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and the site has no sign-up, comment, or newsletter forms. We do not ask for your name or email address. The sign finder tool runs in your browser; the birth date you type into it is not sent to us or stored.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply and keep it no longer than needed to handle your message.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like most web hosts, Vercel automatically processes basic technical data for each request, such as IP address, browser type (user agent), referring page, the page requested, and the time of the request. This is used to deliver pages, keep the service secure, and prevent abuse. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Analytics

We do not currently use Google Analytics or any other third-party analytics tool on this site. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the tool is added.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Ads help pay for running the site.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Other third-party vendors and ad networks may also serve ads on this site and use cookies or similar technologies (such as web beacons and device identifiers) to measure and personalize ads.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's advertising technologies page.

Your choices about personalized ads

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google by visiting Google Ads Settings (My Ad Center).
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices (US) or www.youronlinechoices.eu (EU/UK).
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Ads may still appear, but they will be less relevant.

Opting out of personalization does not remove ads; it means the ads you see are not based on your browsing history.

Visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

Where required, we use a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP) to show a consent message so you can accept or refuse advertising cookies before they are used, and you can change your choice later. The legal basis for personalized advertising cookies is your consent; the legal basis for hosting logs is our legitimate interest in running a secure website.

US state privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have the right to know, delete, or correct personal information, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. To exercise a right, use the opt-out links above or email teajawoo@gmail.com. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will change. Significant changes will be noted on this page.
